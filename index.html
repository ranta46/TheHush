Gloamrun · Line 01

The Last Train

In Gloamrun, the night has a face: a white mask, a body of smoke, and one long finger always at its lips.

People call it the Hush. Where it passes, every light and every sound goes out. Tonight it reached Kesmoor.

The last train is leaving. Rook, its conductor, has saved you a seat.
